# Contributing to the Alginte community

Thanks for helping make Alginte better! Alginte itself is **closed source**, so
this repo doesn't accept code contributions — but your bug reports, feature
ideas, and questions are genuinely valuable and shape the roadmap.

## How you can contribute

- **Report a bug.** Open a [Bug report](https://github.com/alginte/community/issues/new/choose).
  A clear reproduction + your Alginte version (from the in-app **About** page)
  and environment gets it fixed fastest.
- **Request a feature.** Open a [Feature request](https://github.com/alginte/community/issues/new/choose).
  Describe the problem you're trying to solve, not only the solution you have in
  mind — it helps us find the best fix.
- **Help others.** Answer questions in [Discussions](https://github.com/alginte/community/discussions).
- **Share feedback.** Post ideas and what you've built in Discussions.

## Before you open an issue

1. **Search** existing [issues](https://github.com/alginte/community/issues?q=is%3Aissue)
   and [discussions](https://github.com/alginte/community/discussions) — it may
   already be reported or answered.
2. **One issue per report.** Separate unrelated bugs into separate issues.
3. **Security issues are different** — do **not** file them publicly; follow
   [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).

## What to expect

This is maintained by a small team, so responses may take a little time. Issues
are triaged and labeled; a filed issue isn't a commitment to implement, but
everything is read and considered.

By participating you agree to the [Code of Conduct](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
